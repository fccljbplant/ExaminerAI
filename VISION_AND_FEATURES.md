## Summary

TraineesAI is positioned to be better than Skool, Duolingo, and Discord — combined — for industry training.

**The unique position:** AI trains at scale + human mentors at the edges. No competitor does this.

### Upcoming phases:

| Phase | Focus | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 6** | Gamification | XP, levels, streaks, badges, leagues, community feed |
| **Phase 7** | Social learning | Study groups, peer code review, live sessions |
| **Phase 8** | AI personalization | Learning paths, targeted practice, mentor briefings |
| **Phase 9** | Career prep | Portfolio auto-gen, mock interviews, certifications |
| **Phase 10** | Mobile | PWA, voice accessibility |
| **Phase 11** | Analytics | Student, instructor, institution dashboards |
| **Phase 12** | Monetization | Pricing, viral growth, content marketplace |
